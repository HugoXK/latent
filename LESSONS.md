# Lessons

Dated, open constraints. A lesson stays open until a decision or a test closes it.
Do not delete a lesson; mark it closed with the reason.

## 2026-11-25 — Generation is commoditised

Any model can produce twenty ideas. The defensible part is the ledger, the gates,
the compiler and the memory. **Constraint:** no feature may be justified by "the
model writes better text".

## 2026-11-25 — Novelty is not value

LLM ideas are often judged more novel but less feasible. A surprising idea that
cannot be built is a liability. **Constraint:** every gate names a falsifier and a
fallback; feasibility is not optional.

## 2026-11-25 — Diversity collapses at the set level

LLM product ideas are less novel per idea and less diverse per set. A prompt that
says "be diverse" is not enough. **Constraint:** the reconstruction always produces
variants (conservative / ambitious / cross-domain) and runs an adversarial critic.

## 2026-11-25 — Reconstruction from fragments is hard

A blind benchmark recovers a true research idea from a pre-publication bibliography
only ~3–15% of the time. **Constraint:** never promise precise reconstruction. Show
1–3 hypotheses, an assumption ledger and a confidence.

## 2026-11-25 — Under-specification is the real gap

A novel idea can be too vague to implement. Codification readiness is a separate
capability. **Constraint:** the compiler is a first-class product; a brief that a
coding agent cannot act on is not done.

## 2026-11-25 — Evaluation is hard and human

Experts disagree substantially on idea quality. **Constraint:** use panels and
pairwise comparison; report disagreement; do not ship a single judge as truth.

## 2026-11-25 — The artifact is the unit

Fledge's lesson: optimise for the artifact a reviewer needs (one PR), not the demo.
For Latent the artifact is one brief or spec, with its provenance. **Constraint:**
no feature ships without a named artifact and a validator.
