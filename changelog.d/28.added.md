interviewer: calibrate option priors from outcome-backed answers

`observationsFromOutcomes` reads only ideas with an outcome, one observation per
idea and question (a corrected outcome replaces the earlier one). Once a question
has `min_samples` (default 5) matching answers, `questionPriors` replaces the
declared prior with a smoothed empirical distribution; otherwise it is uniform.
The module is exported from the `@latent/core` package root; the sample count and
source are exposed on every question prior.
