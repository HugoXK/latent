brief: render collision evidence and distinguish "not checked"

`BriefCollision` can carry `claim_ids` and `source_url`; `Brief` records
`collision_check` (`checked`/`not_checked`). Markdown and HTML always show the
novelty status, so a missing check is not rendered as clean. `latent novelty`
builds a collision report from prior art, and `latent brief --collisions <file>`
passes the evidence into the compiler.
