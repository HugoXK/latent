runtime: add the FlyBot-compatible HttpAgentRuntime and signed callbacks

`HttpAgentRuntime` schedules and cancels jobs over a small HTTP contract with a
runtime-scoped token and an idempotency key. `runtimeFromEnv` fails closed on a
partial configuration; the CLI records a failed job on runtime errors. Callback
signing/verification is job-scoped (HMAC over job id, timestamp and raw body) and
tested against a recorded contract fixture. Live FlyBot execution remains an
operator integration.
