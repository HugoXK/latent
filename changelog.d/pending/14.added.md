release: add a manual prepare/publish workflow with an SBOM and provenance

`release.yml` verifies a green main, `release.py` folds fragments and bumps
versions into a reviewable PR, and `sbom.py` builds a deterministic CycloneDX 1.5
document. Publish refuses remaining fragments or a version mismatch, tags the
verified main commit, attaches the SBOM and source archive, and records provenance.
