incubator: propose budgeted connections and resurface old fragments

`planIncubation` scores term overlap deterministically, skips existing edges and
suggestions, and never exceeds its budget. Jobs run through the `AgentRuntime` port
and produce reviewable `Suggestion` records (`accept|reject|revoke`). The CLI adds
`incubate`, `suggestions`, `suggestion` and a read-only `digest`; migration 4 adds
owner-scoped jobs and suggestions with a four-job cap.
